<h2 id="the-problem"><span class="lang-en lang-inline">The Problem</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">问题所在</span></h2>

<p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">Generating a video with a diffusion model is expensive. The model starts from noise and denoises the clip step by step, over a long sequence of spatiotemporal tokens, so a single request can keep a GPU busy for seconds to minutes. Serving video generation at scale means paying that cost over and over.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">用扩散模型生成一段视频很贵。模型从噪声出发、沿着一长串时空 token 逐步去噪，一个请求就能让 GPU 忙上数秒到数分钟。而要对外提供服务，这份开销要一遍又一遍地付。</span></p>

<p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">There is an obvious source of savings hiding in the traffic. Real workloads are repetitive: many requests ask for broadly similar things — the same kind of scene, the same objects, the same layout — so the computation behind one finished request could help answer a later one. Reusing work across requests is called <strong>cross-request reuse</strong>.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">而真实流量里藏着一个显而易见的省算力机会：请求是重复的。大量请求要的是大体相似的东西 —— 同类场景、同样的物体、相似的构图 —— 那么一个已完成请求背后的计算，本可以用来回答之后的请求。这种跨请求搬运计算的做法，叫做<strong>跨请求复用</strong>。</span></p>

<p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">The hard part is knowing when reuse is <em>safe</em>. Existing methods decide it from <strong>prompt similarity</strong>: if the text of the new request is close enough to a past one, reuse its cache. But similar prompts do not guarantee similar videos. Two prompts that read almost the same can drift into completely different visual structure as denoising proceeds, so a cache accepted on prompt similarity alone can quietly carry the wrong scene into the result — and the final quality score may not even reveal it.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">难的是判断什么时候复用<em>安全</em>。现有方法靠<strong>提示词相似度</strong>来决定：只要新请求的文本和历史上某个请求足够接近，就复用它的缓存。但提示词相似并不等于视频相似。两句读起来几乎一样的话，在去噪过程中可能演化出完全不同的画面结构 —— 于是仅凭提示词相似度接受的缓存，会把错误的场景悄无声息地带进结果，而最终的质量指标甚至看不出来。</span></p>

<figure class="paper-figure">
  <img src="/publications/paper_imgs/carnator/motivation.jpg" alt="A textually similar cached request still carries the wrong scene" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Figure 1.</strong> Similar text, incompatible generation. </figure>

<p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">That leaves two questions, and Carnator is built around answering both:</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">由此留下两个问题，Carnator 正是围绕它们设计的：</span></p>

<ol class="paper-steps">
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Reuse validity.</strong> Is this historical computation actually compatible with the request we are serving right now?</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>复用有效性。</strong>这段历史计算，与当前正在服务的请求真的兼容吗？</span></li>
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Reuse scope.</strong> If it is, which parts of the computation can we skip — and which parts must still be computed for the new request?</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>复用范围。</strong>如果兼容，哪些计算可以省掉？哪些又必须为新请求重新算？</span></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="key-idea"><span class="lang-en lang-inline">Key Idea</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">核心思路</span></h2>

<p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">Carnator stops guessing from the text and looks inside the model instead.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">Carnator 不再从文本去猜，而是往模型内部看。</span></p>

<div class="paper-callout">
  <span class="paper-callout__label"><span class="lang-en lang-inline">The insight</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">核心洞见</span></span>
  <p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">Prompt similarity is only a <em>proxy</em> for compatibility. The generation process itself is the real signal.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">提示词相似度只是兼容性的<em>代理指标</em>，真正的信号在生成过程本身。</span></p>
  <p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">Early in denoising, the partially formed state already reveals how the clip is taking shape — where objects are appearing, how large they are, what they look like. Carnator runs a <strong>lightweight early probe</strong>, summarises that evidence into a compact <strong>Early Signature</strong>, and compares signatures instead of prompts.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">在去噪早期，尚未成形的中间状态就已经透露出这段视频将长成什么样 —— 物体出现在哪里、有多大、是什么样子。Carnator 执行一次<strong>轻量的早期探测</strong>，把这部分证据压缩成一个紧凑的<strong>早期签名（Early Signature）</strong>，然后比较签名，而不是比较提示词。</span></p>
  <p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">Because the same evidence also marks <em>where</em> the target-specific content sits, it answers the second question too: reuse everywhere the generations agree, and recompute only the regions that genuinely differ.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">同一份证据还标出了<em>目标特有内容位于何处</em>，于是第二个问题也一并有了答案：凡两次生成一致的地方就复用，只重算真正不同的区域。</span></p>
</div>

  <img src="/publications/paper_imgs/carnator/early-signature.jpg" alt="Early denoising states separate compatible from incompatible request pairs" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Figure 2.</strong> The signal is there early. Similar request pairs line up in their opening denoising representations, while easy and hard negatives drift apart in prompt-conditioned residuals and latent dynamics — which is what makes a short, cheap probe enough to tell them apart.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>图 2。</strong>信号在早期就已经出现。相似的请求对在最初几步的去噪表示上高度对齐，而「易负例」与「难负例」则在提示词条件下的残差与隐动态上依次偏离 —— 这就是一次短而廉价的探测足以区分它们的原因。</span></figcaption>
</figure>

<h2 id="how-it-works"><span class="lang-en lang-inline">How It Works</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">方法</span></h2>

<figure class="paper-figure">
  <img src="/publications/paper_imgs/2026-09-22-Carnator.png" alt="Overview of Carnator">
  <figcaption><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Figure 3.</strong> The pipeline end to end. Historical requests are recalled by text, filtered with generation-native Early Signatures, and — for an accepted cache — reused jointly across latent trajectories and sparse attention connectivity, while target-specific computation is recomputed.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>图 3。</strong>整体流程。历史请求先按文本召回，再用生成原生的早期签名过滤；对通过筛选的缓存，同时复用其隐变量轨迹与稀疏注意力连接，同时重算目标特有的部分。</span></figcaption>
</figure>

<ol class="paper-steps">
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Probe early, not at the end.</strong> A short and cheap probe of the target's early denoising states builds an Early Signature. For each object named in the prompt it records a spatiotemporal support mask, the object's relative size, and its mean CIELAB colour.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>在早期探测，而不是等到最后。</strong>对目标请求的早期去噪状态做一次短而廉价的探测，构建早期签名：为提示词里每个物体记录时空支撑掩码、相对尺寸与平均 CIELAB 颜色。</span></li>
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Filter under a risk budget.</strong> The target signature is compared with recalled candidates on four discrepancies — context appearance, object size, spatiotemporal shape, and object appearance. The thresholds are calibrated to maximise reuse coverage subject to a bound on unsafe reuses, then certified on a held-out set with a one-sided Clopper&ndash;Pearson bound. If nothing passes, the request simply falls back to full generation.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>在风险预算下筛选。</strong>目标签名与召回候选在四个差异维度上比较：上下文外观、物体尺寸、时空形状、物体外观。阈值在校准集上以「最大化复用覆盖率、同时约束不安全复用比例」为目标选取，再在一个独立认证集上用单侧 Clopper&ndash;Pearson 上界做认证。若没有候选通过，请求直接退回完整生成。</span></li>
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Reuse along two axes at once.</strong> Once a cache is accepted, <em>region-guided latent reuse</em> recomputes tokens inside the evidence-derived active region and inherits the rest from the historical trajectory, while <em>historical sparse-connectivity reuse</em> decides <em>where</em> attention is evaluated — transferring the historical attention structure, not the historical attention values. The two are combined in the middle Transformer layers.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>沿两个维度同时复用。</strong>缓存被接受后，<em>区域引导的隐变量复用</em>只对证据推导出的活跃区域重算 token，其余从历史轨迹继承；<em>历史稀疏连接复用</em>则决定注意力<em>在哪里</em>计算 —— 迁移的是历史的注意力结构，而不是历史的注意力数值。两者在中间层 Transformer 中结合使用。</span></li>
</ol>

<h3><span class="lang-en lang-inline">What the numbers say</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">这些数字说明了什么</span></h3>

<ol class="paper-steps">
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>It wins by being pickier, not looser.</strong> Adding the Early Signature lowers the hit rate from 77.33% to 72.67%: roughly one in twenty textually similar caches is rejected as generation-incompatible. Latency barely moves (185.6&nbsp;s to 187.30&nbsp;s) while CLIP-T rises from 0.2725 to 0.2913 and VBench-Q from 0.6301 to 0.6382 — the filter removes caches that were hurting alignment, and costs almost nothing.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>它是靠更挑剔取胜，而不是更宽松。</strong>加入早期签名后命中率从 77.33% 降到 72.67%：大约每二十个文本相似的缓存里就有一个被判定为「生成不兼容」而拒绝。而延迟几乎没变（185.6&nbsp;s → 187.30&nbsp;s），CLIP-T 反而从 0.2725 升到 0.2913、VBench-Q 从 0.6301 升到 0.6382 —— 这个过滤器剔除的正是原本在损害文本对齐的缓存，代价几乎为零。</span></li>
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Fewer accepted caches, larger savings each.</strong> On Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, Carnator accepts fewer caches than the baselines yet still delivers the fastest cache-hit latency, dropping it from 1138.6&nbsp;s (dense) to 525.58&nbsp;s. Joint reuse extracts more computation out of each cache it accepts.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>接受的缓存更少，但每个缓存的收益更大。</strong>在 Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B 上，Carnator 接受的缓存比基线更少，缓存命中延迟却是最低的：从稠密生成的 1138.6&nbsp;s 降到 525.58&nbsp;s。联合复用的意义就在于，从每一个被接受的缓存里榨出更多可省的计算。</span></li>
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>Quality is preserved, not traded away.</strong> On the primary model, VBench-Q is 0.6382 against 0.6331 for dense generation, and CLIP-T stays in the same range as the baselines.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>质量是被保住的，不是拿来交换的。</strong>在主要评测模型上，VBench-Q 为 0.6382，稠密生成是 0.6331；CLIP-T 也保持在基线的同一区间。</span></li>
  <li><span class="lang-en lang-inline"><strong>The two reuse axes are complementary.</strong> On a fixed 100-request subset, latent reuse alone reaches 206.06&nbsp;s and attention reuse alone 160.91&nbsp;s, but combining them reaches 155.85&nbsp;s (1.71×) — better than either on its own.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline"><strong>两个复用维度是互补的。</strong>在固定的 100 条请求子集上，只用隐变量复用是 206.06&nbsp;s，只用注意力复用是 160.91&nbsp;s，两者结合则是 155.85&nbsp;s（1.71×）—— 优于任何单独一项。</span></li>
</ol>

<p><span class="lang-en lang-inline">On a disjoint certification set, the risk-aware rule accepts 50 of 70 request-cache pairs with no unsafe cases, giving a one-sided Clopper&ndash;Pearson upper bound of 4.50% and satisfying the prescribed 5% risk constraint.</span><span class="lang-zh lang-inline">在独立的认证集上，风险感知规则在 70 对「请求&ndash;缓存」中接受了 50 对，且没有出现不安全案例，单侧 Clopper&ndash;Pearson 置信上界为 4.50%，满足预设的 5% 风险约束。</span></p>
